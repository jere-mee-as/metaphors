# Mind & knowing | ◉
## The mind | ◉
### The mind is a container | MW | Thoughts sit inside the head, go in, come out, and fill it up.
- I can't get that song out of my head.
- His head is full of nonsense.
- Keep that in mind.
- It just popped into my head.
- Her mind is empty after exams.
### The mind is a machine | MW | Thinking runs, breaks down, gears up and runs out of steam.
- My brain isn't working today.
- I'm a little rusty on calculus.
- You can see the wheels turning.
- She's running out of steam.
- He's churning out ideas.
- My mind just shut down.
### The mind is a brittle object | MW | Minds can crack, shatter and fall apart under strain.
- She cracked under the pressure.
- He's been shattered since the news.
- I'm going to pieces.
- Handle him gently, he's fragile right now.
- Her mind snapped.
### The mind is a body | PF | Thinking is done with the mind's hands, eyes, legs and stomach.
- I can't stomach that argument.
- Let me chew on it.
- She has a nimble mind.
- He has a firm grip on the theory.
### The society of mind | PF | The self is split into parts that argue, bargain and overrule one another.
- Part of me wants to go.
- I'm of two minds about it.
- I had to talk myself into it.
- My better judgment lost.
### Memory is a storehouse | ML | Memories are stored, filed, retrieved and lost.
- Let me dig that up.
- It's stored somewhere in the back of my mind.
- I can't retrieve her name.
- That memory got buried.
- It's on the tip of my tongue.
## Ideas | ✦
### Ideas are objects | MW | Ideas can be handed over, held, dropped and turned around.
- Let me give you an idea.
- Turn that idea over a few times.
- She picked up the thought where he dropped it.
- He tossed out a suggestion.
### Ideas are food | MW | Ideas are cooked, swallowed, digested and found tasteless.
- That's half-baked.
- I can't swallow that claim.
- Let me digest this before I answer.
- There's a lot to chew on here.
- It's warmed-over nonsense.
- She devoured the book.
- That leaves a bad taste in my mouth.
### Ideas are people | MW | Ideas are born, grow up, have fathers and die.
- He's the father of modern genetics.
- Behaviorism died in the 60s.
- That theory gave birth to a whole field.
- The idea is still in its infancy.
- Those ideas will live on.
### Ideas are plants | MW | Ideas grow, bloom, branch out and bear fruit.
- The project finally bore fruit.
- The idea took root.
- It has branched out into three fields.
- That's the seed of a better plan.
- Her work is the offshoot of his.
### Ideas are products | MW | Ideas are manufactured, refined and turned out.
- We produce a lot of ideas in these meetings.
- That needs refining.
- He turns out a paper a month.
- It's still a rough draft of a thought.
### Ideas are commodities | MW | Ideas are bought, sold, packaged and marketed.
- I'm not buying it.
- That idea won't sell.
- He's packaged it nicely.
- It's a worthless notion.
### Ideas are resources | MW | Ideas get used up, wasted and drawn on.
- I've run out of ideas.
- Let's pool our ideas.
- That's a waste of a good thought.
- She has a deep well of material.
### Ideas are money | MW | Ideas are a wealth to save, invest and spend.
- She has a rich store of knowledge.
- He's invested a lot in that theory.
- Add your two cents.
- That's a priceless insight.
### Ideas are cutting instruments | MW | Ideas are sharp, cut to the heart or are blunt.
- That's an incisive point.
- Her remark cut right through the noise.
- He has a razor-sharp mind.
- The argument is a bit blunt.
### Ideas are fashions | MW | Ideas come into style and go out of date.
- That theory is out of fashion.
- Rationalism is back in style.
- That's very dated thinking.
- It's the trendy view right now.
### Ideas are light-sources | MW | Ideas illuminate, shed light and are brilliant.
- That idea lit up the room.
- Her paper sheds light on the problem.
- It was a brilliant insight.
- He's a bright student.
### Discourse is a light-medium | MW | Clear prose lets the light through; muddy prose blocks it.
- That explanation is crystal clear.
- His argument is murky.
- The essay is transparent about its aims.
- That paragraph is opaque.
## Understanding | ◎
### Understanding is seeing | MW | To understand is to see; to explain is to show.
- I see what you mean.
- Look at it from my point of view.
- That's a clear argument.
- Now it's obvious.
- What's your outlook?
- He's blind to the problem.
### Knowing is seeing | PF | Knowledge comes from looking; ignorance is blindness or darkness.
- I'll see what I can find out.
- We're in the dark about the budget.
- She's a visionary.
- Let me have a look at the data.
- That's a blind spot for him.
### Understanding is grasping | MW | Understanding is taking hold of an idea with the hands.
- I can't grasp the concept.
- That's over my head.
- She has a firm grasp of statistics.
- It slipped right through my fingers.
- He's out of his depth.
### Thinking is moving | PF | Thought travels: reasoning goes somewhere, gets sidetracked and reaches conclusions.
- Where was I going with this?
- We reached the same conclusion.
- Let me back up a step.
- You're jumping to conclusions.
- I followed his reasoning.
- That's a roundabout argument.
### Thinking is object manipulation | PF | Ideas are handled, weighed, put together and sorted.
- Let's put our heads together.
- Weigh both options.
- She juggles a lot of ideas.
- He toyed with the idea.
- Let's sort this out.
### Thinking is perceiving | PF | Ideas are seen, heard, smelled and felt.
- That sounds right.
- Something smells fishy here.
- I'm getting a feel for it.
- Did you catch that?
### Acquiring ideas is eating | PF | Learning is feeding; teaching is spoon-feeding.
- He spoon-feeds his students.
- She soaks up everything.
- I'm hungry for knowledge.
- That's a lot to take in.
### Thinking about something is examining it | WF | To think about something is to look into it closely.
- Let's look into it.
- She looked over the proposal.
- Take a closer look at chapter two.
- I'll keep an eye on it.
## Reason | Λ
### Rational is up; emotional is down | MW | Reason sits above feeling; emotions drag discussions down.
- The talk fell to an emotional level.
- Rise above your feelings.
- Let's keep it on a high plane.
- He couldn't rise above his anger.
### Reason is a force | PF | Reasons push and pull; arguments compel.
- That argument is compelling.
- I was forced to conclude it.
- The evidence drove us to that view.
- She was swayed by the data.
### Logic is a path | PF | Inference is a road; valid steps stay on it.
- That doesn't follow.
- Your argument goes off the rails.
- It's a logical step.
- You've gone in a circle.
- That leads straight to a contradiction.
### Theories are buildings | MW | Theories have foundations, frameworks and supports; they stand or collapse.
- What is the theory founded on?
- That's a shaky claim.
- It needs more evidence to prop it up.
- The whole theory collapsed.
- She constructed a strong case.
- That framework needs shoring up.
### Problems are puzzles | MW | Problems have a solution that fits; pieces snap into place.
- I've solved it.
- The pieces are starting to fit.
- We're missing a piece.
- It's a real brainteaser.
### Problems are precipitates in a chemical solution | MW | Problems dissolve but may come back out of solution.
- That problem dissolved after we talked.
- It keeps coming back.
- The issue crystallized last week.
- That's a catalyst for trouble.
### Problems are solid objects | MW | Problems are hard, heavy and in the way.
- That's a hard problem.
- It's a weighty issue.
- We tackled it head on.
- The obstacle is still there.

- She finally found herself.
- He's in touch with who he really is.
### The self is a container | PF | The self holds things in and lets them out.
- I'm bottling it all up.
- She opened up to me.
- He's an empty shell.
